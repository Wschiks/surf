2. Free to play and the shop

The game is free to play. In the phone apps short ad breaks show every few minutes (never in the first minutes of play), and you can choose to watch video ads for bonuses (gems, coins x2, the rescue helicopter's gift). In the shop you can buy optional items: packs of gems (as often as you like), coins for gems, treasure boxes, and two one-time items, "Remove ads" and "Coins x5". Prices are shown in the shop in your own currency. Purchases are paid through the App Store or Google Play, are for your own use on your own account, and the one-time items can be restored with "Restore purchases" in the shop. Gems that were bought and spent cannot be restored or refunded by us. Coins, gems, skins and everything else in the game have no value outside the game and cannot be exchanged for money or traded.

3. Treasure boxes and chance

A treasure box gives one piece of a skin; which skin is drawn by chance. The odds are shown in the shop before you buy, behind the "?" of each box. A normal box: Common 50%, Rare 36%, Epic 10%, Legendary 4%. A golden box: Common 0%, Rare 14%, Epic 33%, Legendary 53%. Boxes can be earned for free (quests, new sections, the daily calendar, the rescue helicopter, the Surf Club) or bought: the coin box with in-game coins (50,000 coins for the first, each next one costs 3 times more), 3 boxes for 20 gems, a golden box for 40 gems. The draw is fixed when the box is opened and is saved, so it cannot be changed by reloading. Surf Club skins never come from boxes. The rescue helicopter's gift and when it comes are also decided by chance. Nothing else in the game is random.

4. The Surf Club

The Surf Club is a monthly subscription (price shown in the shop, in your own currency). For as long as it is active it gives coins x2, ad rewards without watching ads and no ad breaks, 5 gems and 1 treasure box once every 24 hours (claimed in the shop; missed days do not add up), 2 extra hours of away time, and a Surf Club skin each month: on the 1st of the month, or right away when you join, you get that month's skin. A month's skin is only given during that month to members; a missed month is not given later. Skins you have received stay yours after the subscription ends. The subscription renews automatically every month until you cancel it in the settings of your App Store or Google Play account (cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge); you keep the benefits until the paid period ends. We cannot cancel it for you. Refunds follow the rules of the store you bought from.

5. Daily rewards

The daily rewards calendar gives one reward for each day you play, and a new calendar starts on the 1st of each month. It uses the date of your device.

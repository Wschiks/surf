# Surf Tycoon: every sport, every section

Every sport has 4 sections, from easy to hard. Each section must look and feel different: other riders, other gear, other water, another speed and its own **signature move**, the thing you notice straight away.

Speeds in this file: **slow**, **medium**, **fast**, **very fast**.

---

## 1. Wave surfing

### 1.1 School
- **What it is:** a surf class. Kids on soft foam boards in the white water close to the beach.
- **Riders:** kids in bright foam vests; an instructor standing waist-deep in the water.
- **Gear:** wide, soft foam boards with a coloured top (blue, yellow).
- **Water:** small broken white water rolling in. No clean waves.
- **Movement:** **slow**.
  - The instructor pushes a kid into the foam.
  - The kid rides straight to the beach, lying down or on the knees; some stand up, wobbly, arms wide.
  - Lots of falls. They walk back out next to the board.
- **Signature:** the instructor pushing a kid into the white water.

### 1.2 Longboard
- **What it is:** surfers on long boards on small, long, rolling waves. Style, not speed.
- **Riders:** relaxed surfers in boardshorts and soft-coloured rash vests.
- **Gear:** long, mostly soft boards (about 1.5 times a normal surfboard), round nose, soft colours.
- **Water:** small, smooth, long lines of swell that peel slowly. Little foam.
- **Movement:** **slow**.
  - Long, calm rides along the wave with a few wide turns.
  - The rider **walks to the nose and back** (cross-stepping) and puts the toes over the nose (hang five).
  - Sometimes two riders share one wave.
- **Signature:** walking to the nose of the board.

### 1.3 Reef
- **What it is:** good surfers on fast, hollow waves breaking over a shallow reef. The waves **barrel** (they curl into a tube).
- **Riders:** good surfers in wetsuits.
- **Gear:** short white shortboards.
- **Water:** a clear wave that peels along the reef, with a curling white lip; the reef colours show through.
- **Movement:** **fast**.
  - Late take-off, then a bottom turn.
  - Into the **barrel**: half hidden under the curl.
  - Out with a burst of spray, then a snap or cutback.
  - Sometimes the barrel closes on them: wipeout.
- **Signature:** riding inside the barrel and shooting out of it.

### 1.4 Nazaré
- **What it is:** the biggest waves in the world. **Big, big waves that barrel.** Surfers are towed in by a jet ski.
- **Riders:** pros in black suits, helmets and orange impact vests; jet ski drivers.
- **Gear:** long, narrow big-wave boards with foot straps; red jet skis with a tow rope.
- **Water:** one or two **giant waves** at a time with huge foam explosions; calm, dark water between them.
- **Movement:** **very fast**.
  - The jet ski pulls the surfer onto the wave; the surfer lets go.
  - A huge drop straight down.
  - A giant barrel, then out in a cloud of spray (or a huge wipeout).
  - The jet ski picks the surfer up and drives back out.
- **Signature:** the jet ski tow-in and the giant barrel.

## 2. Skimboarding

### 2.1 Shallows
- **What it is:** first-timers throwing a board on ankle-deep water at the water's edge.
- **Riders:** kids in T-shirts and swimsuits.
- **Gear:** round wooden boards.
- **Water:** a thin layer of water on the sand, no waves.
- **Movement:** **slow**. Short run, short wobbly glide, often a fall on the bum, then walk back.
- **Signature:** the fall on the bum.

### 2.2 Flatland
- **What it is:** tricks on flat wet sand with a thin layer of water.
- **Riders:** teenagers in boardshorts and caps.
- **Gear:** small, light foam boards in bright colours.
- **Water:** wide, flat, shiny wet sand.
- **Movement:** **medium**.
  - Long glides along the beach, one trick per glide.
  - Tricks: a 360 spin, a shove-it (only the board spins under the rider), or a body varial (only the rider spins).
- **Signature:** the board spinning under the rider.

### 2.3 Shore break
- **What it is:** the real skimboarding. Glide out, hit a small wave, turn on it and ride back to the sand.
- **Riders:** wave riders in boardshorts and rash vests.
- **Gear:** blue foam skimboards.
- **Water:** small, steep waves breaking close to the sand.
- **Movement:** **fast**. Fast run, long glide out, a sharp turn on the wave with a fan of spray, ride the wave back in.
- **Signature:** the turn on the wave.

### 2.4 Big shore break
- **What it is:** pros on big waves that break right onto the sand. They fly off the top of the wave.
- **Riders:** pros in tight wetsuits.
- **Gear:** dark carbon boards.
- **Water:** big, hollow waves crashing on the sand, lots of foam.
- **Movement:** **very fast**. Hit the wave and **fly off the lip** with a spin in the air, land in the foam, or a big wipeout.
- **Signature:** flying off the top of the wave.

## 3. Windsurfing

### 3.1 School
- **What it is:** a windsurf class. Standing upright, sailing slowly, falling in a lot.
- **Riders:** kids and adults in foam vests; an instructor in a small rubber boat.
- **Gear:** very wide, stable boards; small sails.
- **Water:** shallow, flat water, light wind.
- **Movement:** **slow**.
  - Short, stiff runs back and forth.
  - Turn by walking around the mast.
  - Often fall in, then pull the sail out of the water with the rope.
- **Signature:** the sail lying on the water and being pulled up.

### 3.2 Slalom (was Freeride)
- **What it is:** **speed.** Windsurf racing at full speed between yellow buoys.
- **Riders:** racers in harnesses, with numbers on their sails.
- **Gear:** narrow, fast race boards; big race sails.
- **Water:** strong wind, slightly choppy water, yellow buoys.
- **Movement:** **very fast**.
  - Always planing: feet in the straps, hooked into the harness, leaning far back, the board just skimming the water.
  - Long trails of spray and wake.
  - A fast gybe round every buoy, and racing and overtaking each other.
- **Signature:** full speed with the board flying over the water.

### 3.3 Freestyle (was Speed and freestyle)
- **What it is:** **tricks.** Jumps, spins and moves with the board in the air.
- **Riders:** young trick riders in bright wetsuits.
- **Gear:** short, small freestyle boards; light, bright sails.
- **Water:** strong wind, choppy water.
- **Movement:** **medium**.
  - Short runs, then a trick:
    - A **jump with the board high in the air**: the rider almost upside down, the board above the head.
    - **Vulcan:** pop up, turn 180° in the air, land going the other way.
    - **Spock:** a Vulcan that keeps spinning flat on the water.
    - **Flaka:** the same kind of spin, but turning upwind.
    - **Sailing backwards.**
  - A splash on every landing.
- **Signature:** the jump with the board high in the air.

### 3.4 Wave zone
- **What it is:** pros in strong wind and big waves. Jumping off the waves and riding them.
- **Riders:** pros in black suits.
- **Gear:** small wave boards; small, strong sails.
- **Water:** strong wind, big breaking waves.
- **Movement:** **fast**.
  - Going out: big jumps off every wave, sometimes a full loop.
  - Coming back: ride the wave like a surfer, with sharp turns and spray.
- **Signature:** the high jump off a wave.

## 4. Kitesurfing

### 4.1 Kite school
- **What it is:** beginners learning to fly the kite, then dragged through the water, then their first short rides.
- **Riders:** beginners in vests and helmets; an instructor next to them.
- **Gear:** small trainer kites, school kites, big wide boards.
- **Water:** shallow, flat water, steady wind.
- **Movement:** **slow**.
  - Some fly a small kite on the beach.
  - Some are pulled through the water without a board.
  - Some ride a few metres and fall, with the kite crashing on the water.
- **Signature:** the kite crashing on the water.

### 4.2 Freeride
- **What it is:** relaxed kiting, cruising back and forth.
- **Riders:** riders in boardshorts and rash vests.
- **Gear:** twin-tip boards, kites in many colours.
- **Water:** steady wind, slightly choppy water.
- **Movement:** **medium**. Long, smooth runs, carving turns at the end with spray, a small hop now and then.
- **Signature:** the smooth carve.

### 4.3 Freestyle and big air
- **What it is:** tricks and jumps.
- **Riders:** young trick riders with helmets and impact vests.
- **Gear:** small boards with boots.
- **Water:** strong wind, choppy water, lots of open space.
- **Movement:** **fast**.
  - A trick on every run: the body flying out flat behind (a raley), passing the bar behind the back in the air, jumps with a spin.
  - A splash on landing.
- **Signature:** the body flying out flat behind the kite.

### 4.4 Big air and waves
- **What it is:** pros in very strong wind. Huge jumps and riding big waves.
- **Riders:** pros in black suits, helmets and impact vests.
- **Gear:** twin-tips for jumping; surfboards without straps for the waves.
- **Water:** very strong wind, big waves.
- **Movement:** **very fast**.
  - Huge, long jumps; the kite sometimes makes a full loop in the air (kiteloop).
  - In the waves: riding like a surfer with the kite overhead.
- **Signature:** the kiteloop.

## 5. Foil and wing

### 5.1 Foil school
- **What it is:** beginners trying to get the board out of the water.
- **Riders:** beginners in vests and helmets.
- **Gear:** big, thick, wide boards; small school wings.
- **Water:** flat, calm water.
- **Movement:** **slow**. Mostly on the water; short hops of 1–2 seconds, then back down with a splash. Lots of falls.
- **Signature:** the short hop and splash.

### 5.2 Wing freeride
- **What it is:** flying smoothly above the water with a wing in the hands.
- **Riders:** riders in wetsuits or boardshorts.
- **Gear:** normal wing boards, wings in many colours.
- **Water:** light wind, flat water.
- **Movement:** **medium**. Long, smooth curves high above the water, the wing passing over the head in the turns.
- **Signature:** the smooth flying curve.

### 5.3 Downwind
- **What it is:** experienced riders gliding from swell to swell, with the wind behind them.
- **Riders:** experienced riders.
- **Gear:** long, narrow boards; the wing held loose above the head.
- **Water:** open water with long, rolling swell.
- **Movement:** **medium**. A slow, smooth glide with the swells in gentle S-curves, then a fast ride back upwind.
- **Signature:** gliding on the swells with the wing loose overhead.

### 5.4 Pro arena
- **What it is:** pros doing jumps and spins.
- **Riders:** pros in black suits, helmets and impact vests.
- **Gear:** small, short boards; small, strong wings.
- **Water:** big waves, strong wind.
- **Movement:** **fast**. High jumps with spins and flips, carving on the waves.
- **Signature:** the jump with a spin high above the water.

## 6. Sailing

### 6.1 Sailing school
- **What it is:** kids in tiny dinghies with an instructor boat.
- **Riders:** kids in yellow life vests; an instructor in a rubber boat.
- **Gear:** small, square-nosed dinghies (Optimists) with one little sail.
- **Water:** sheltered, flat water, light wind.
- **Movement:** **slow**. A line of little boats behind the instructor, like ducklings. Clumsy turns; sometimes one tips over and is pulled back up.
- **Signature:** the line of little boats, and tipping over.

### 6.2 Boat hire
- **What it is:** weekend sailors in hired boats, sailing wherever they like.
- **Riders:** couples and families, two people per boat.
- **Gear:** a mix of dinghies and catamarans (two hulls).
- **Water:** open water near the shore, steady wind.
- **Movement:** **medium**. Wandering everywhere, sometimes stopping with flapping sails. Catamarans are faster and sometimes lift one hull out of the water.
- **Signature:** the catamaran with one hull in the air.

### 6.3 Club racing
- **What it is:** a real race on a course with buoys.
- **Riders:** racing sailors with numbers on their sails.
- **Gear:** fast racing dinghies; a committee boat with a flag at the start line.
- **Water:** open water, strong wind, three race buoys.
- **Movement:** **fast**.
  - Waiting behind the start line, then everyone starts at the same moment.
  - Zig-zag upwind to the first buoy, around the course, then back to the start for the next race.
- **Signature:** the mass start.

### 6.4 Offshore regatta
- **What it is:** pro crews in big racing yachts on the open sea.
- **Riders:** 4–6 crew members per boat in red or black jackets.
- **Gear:** big racing yachts with a big, colourful balloon sail at the front (spinnaker).
- **Water:** open sea, strong wind, big swell.
- **Movement:** **fast** but heavy.
  - Long legs.
  - Upwind they lean over hard, with spray over the front.
  - Downwind the colourful spinnaker goes up.
  - In the turns the crew runs across the deck.
- **Signature:** the colourful spinnaker.

## Overview

| Sport | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wave surfing | School: slow, white water | Longboard: slow, stylish | Reef: barrels | Nazaré: giant barrels, jet ski |
| Skimboarding | Shallows: falls | Flatland: tricks on the sand | Shore break: turn on the wave | Big shore break: flying off the wave |
| Windsurfing | School: slow, falling in | **Slalom: speed** | **Freestyle: tricks** | Wave zone: jumps off waves |
| Kitesurfing | Kite school: kite crashes | Freeride: cruising | Freestyle and big air: tricks | Big air and waves: kiteloops |
| Foil and wing | Foil school: short hops | Wing freeride: smooth flying | Downwind: gliding on swells | Pro arena: jumps and spins |
| Sailing | School: tiny boats | Boat hire: mixed boats | Club racing: mass start | Offshore regatta: big yachts |
